## Selection conclusion

`NO_SINGLE_DOMINANT_MODEL`. The available evidence supports an operating-condition choice, not a universal champion. Safety-oriented abstention favors the gated rule; raw-score closeness favors additive lambda=0.5; transparent reliability attenuation and explicit missing-evidence status favor the current multiplicative structure. A formal selection claim must wait for common human labels, an outcome holdout, common perturbation/missingness masks, and completed pending challengers.
